The level shifters originally placed in the schematic are not appropriate.  They have a max voltage of 3.6V, and there are signals within the system as high as 12V.

For low-to-high level shifting, a CD40109 may be a suitable part.  

Some power and signal protection has been put in place but needs more consideration.  

Power budget needs to be re-assessed to see if the LM2594 500mA limit is sufficient for every instrument that is planned.  

The TI datasheet for LM2594 has specific recommendations regarding capacitors.  AVX TPS series capacitors is suggested for the output cap.


There should be no direct interfaces to the microcontroller when in normal usage.  All IO and communications should pass through buffers or transcievers, with signal lines protected.

The main concern is lightning induced spikes, and nearby power faults that will damage the equipment.

Reviewing documentation, the LM2594 doesn't actually appear appropriate for this as it doesn't handle the input voltage range and doesn't supply sufficient current at 12V.  Investigating replacing it with the TPS5410.

Seriously considering using LDO for 3V3 and 5V supplies, and having only one buck converter from the 24V supply for cost and simplicity purposes.  Power budget at 3V3 is well within LDO range, likewise for 5V.

The TPS5430 is a 3A rated part, which for a possible load of up to 1A provides a 3:1 rating.  This will improve performance during high temperature events.
